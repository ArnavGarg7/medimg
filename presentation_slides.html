Medical Imaging Triage & Lesion Analysis Workstation

A Triple-Headed Multi-Task SaMD Architecture for Cutaneous Melanoma Screening

Clinical Motivation & Life-Safety Impact
  • Melanoma Mortality: 5-year survival is 99% if detected localized, but collapses to <30% once metastatic.
  • Clinical Bottleneck: Global dermatologist shortages create 8–24 week referral delays. Rapid nodular melanomas expand fatally while waiting for routine skin checks.
  • Objective: Engineer an automated, high-sensitivity triage workstation that pre-screens lesions, outlines boundaries, and detects localized malignant foci in < 200 ms.
Software-as-a-Medical-Device (SaMD) Scope
  • Regulatory Classification: Class II SaMD Clinical Decision Support (CDS) aid under FDA 21 CFR 820.
  • Autonomous Diagnosis? STRICTLY PROHIBITED. Operates with a Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) architecture for specialist second-opinion only.
  • Clinical Target: Sensitivity ≥ 95.0% on malignant melanoma at ≤ 200 ms CPU latency.
  • Verification: 47/47 automated unit tests passing with zero data leakage.
Slide 2 of 7 • Data Curation & Leakage Prevention

Data Engineering & The HAM10000 Patient Leakage Trap

The "Lesion Duplication" Trap
  • Dataset Scale: 10,015 dermatoscopic images from HAM10000 across 7 diagnostic categories (~11% melanoma).
  • The Trap: There are 10,015 images but only 7,470 unique lesions (lesion_id). 1,956 lesions were photographed repeatedly across multiple patient visits (up to 6 times per lesion).
  • Fatal Random Split: A naive train_test_split puts different photos of the same lesion into both train and test. Models memorize patient skin tone and hair rather than disease pathology, resulting in fake 95%+ accuracy that collapses on real patients.
Our Multi-Stage Engineering Defense
  • 1. Grouped Stratification: Partitioning strictly by unique lesion_id into Train (70%), Val (15%), Test (15%). Every image of a lesion belongs to exactly one partition.
  • 2. Perceptual Deduplication: 64-bit difference hashing (dHash): Hamming distance ≤ 12 and pixel MAD ≤ 0.02.
  • 3. Mathematical Audit (pytest):
Lesions_train ∩ Lesions_val = ∅
Lesions_train ∩ Lesions_test = ∅
Lesions_val ∩ Lesions_test = ∅

✓ Zero patient identity leakage mathematically verified.

Slide 3 of 7 • Mathematical Foundations

First-Principles NumPy Core (src/medimg/fundamentals/)

1. Cyclical Jacobi SVD

Implemented 2-sided cyclical Jacobi SVD in pure NumPy without LAPACK.

Orthogonal Givens rotation matrices annihilate off-diagonal elements:

τ = (a_qq - a_pp) / (2 a_pq)
t = sign(τ)/(|τ| + √(1+τ²))
c = 1/√(1+t²), s = t·c

Matches scipy.linalg.svd to within machine precision (< 10-13).

2. Analytic Backprop

Exact vector calculus derivations for Affine layers and Sigmoid BCE loss:

∂L/∂W = X^T (∂L/∂Z)
∂L/∂z = ŷ - y (BCE+Sigmoid)

Verified with central finite differences:

∂L/∂θ ≈ [L(θ+ε) - L(θ-ε)] / 2ε

Matches PyTorch autograd to strictly < 1.0 × 10-9 error.

3. Optimizer Dynamics

Polyak Momentum: Accumulates past velocity v_t:

v_{t+1} = γ v_t + η ∇L(θ_t)
θ_{t+1} = θ_t - v_{t+1}

Nesterov Lookahead: Evaluates gradient at predicted position:

v_{t+1} = γ v_t + η ∇L(θ_t - γ v_t)

Anticipatory brake prevents overshooting steep ravines.

Slide 4 of 7 • Deep Learning Models

Triple-Headed Multi-Task Vision Architecture

1. Triage Classifier

ResNet-18 (ImageNet Pretrained)

11.18M parameters • AdamW (η=10-4)

Residual skip connections \(y = \mathcal{F}(x) + x\) preserve gradient backpropagation through 18 layers.

Class-Weighted BCE Loss: Compensates for 1:8 melanoma imbalance with positive weight = 7.93:

L = -[7.93 y log(ŷ) + (1-y) log(1-ŷ)]

Penalizes false negatives 8× more heavily than false positives.

2. Lesion Segmentor

ResNetUNet (ResNet-34 Encoder)

13.52M parameters • Bilinear Upsampling

Encoder extracts rich hierarchical textures; skip connections concatenate high-res spatial maps to decoder.

Combo Loss Function:

L_Dice = 1 - (2∑pg + ε) / (∑p² + ∑g² + ε)
L_Total = L_BCE + L_Dice

Directly optimizes intersection over union; prevents mask collapse on faint edges.

3. Foci Detector

RetinaNet (ResNet-50 FPN)

32.27M parameters • Feature Pyramid Network

Evaluates > 100,000 candidate anchor boxes; 99.9% are easy background negatives.

Focal Loss (γ = 2.0):

FL(p_t) = -α_t (1 - p_t)² log(p_t)

Downweights easy negatives by up to 10,000×; focuses gradients on ambiguous lesion borders.

Slide 7 of 7 • Failure Audits & Scorecard

Disclosed Failure Audits & Clinical Pilot Scorecard

Disclosed Failure Modes & Limitations
  • 1. Facial Lesion Specificity Drop: Specificity drops to 35.71% on facial skin because chronic solar lentigines and actinic keratoses mimic early melanoma pigment lines.
  • 2. Underexposure Vulnerability: Highly resilient to JPEG compression (down to Q=10) and downscaling, but sensitivity collapses from 95.2% to 81.0% under underexposure (< 0.6×). Mandates calibrated dermatoscope lighting.
  • 3. Rejection of Generative LLMs: An autoregressive Transformer verbalizer hallucinated 92.4% risk as "95.0%". Rejected generative LLMs in favor of a deterministic rule-based verbalizer.
